## Official & Planning

Essential official government entry portals, tourism boards, e-Visa applications, and weather tracking.

- [Vietnam National Authority of Tourism (VNAT)](https://vietnam.travel/) - Official national tourism board portal featuring destination highlights, cultural itineraries, and regional guides.
- [Vietnam Immigration Department (e-Visa Portal)](https://evisa.xuatnhapcanh.gov.vn/) - Official government portal for submitting electronic visa applications for international visitors.
- [National Center for Hydro-Meteorological Forecasting (NCHMF)](https://nchmf.gov.vn/) - Official national meteorological agency providing weather forecasts, typhoon tracking, and seasonal climate reports.

## Transportation & Rail

Navigating national railway networks, sleeper trains, domestic aviation, and intercity buses.

- [Vietnam Railways (Đường sắt Việt Nam - VNR)](https://dsvn.vn/) - National passenger railway operator managing the historic Reunification Express (North-South Railway) connecting Hanoi and Ho Chi Minh City.
- [Vietnam Airlines](https://www.vietnamairlines.com/) - National flag carrier operating international flights and domestic routes across regional airports.
- [Vietjet Air](https://www.vietjetair.com/) - Major domestic and international low-cost airline providing frequent regional connections.
- [Bamboo Airways](https://www.bambooairways.com/) - Domestic carrier offering scheduled flights across major Vietnamese tourism hubs.

## Destinations & Regions

### Hanoi & Northern Highlands

- [Hanoi Department of Tourism](https://vietnam.travel/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring the historic Old Quarter, Hoan Kiem Lake, and French colonial architecture.
- [Sapa Tourism Portal](https://vietnam.travel/) - Regional travel guide for exploring terraced rice fields, Fansipan mountain trekking, and ethnic minority communities.

### Ha Long Bay & Coastal North

- [Ha Long Bay Management Board (UNESCO Heritage Portal)](https://vietnam.travel/) - Official tourism resource for booking licensed boat cruises, limestone karst island tours, and cave explorations.

### Da Nang, Hoi An & Central Coast

- [Da Nang Tourism Promotion Center](https://danangfantasticity.com/) - Official municipal portal for My Khe beach, Marble Mountains, and Ba Na Hills.
- [Hoi An Ancient Town Guide](https://vietnam.travel/) - Official visitor portal for exploring the UNESCO-listed lantern-lit merchant town, Japanese Covered Bridge, and An Bang beach.

### Ho Chi Minh City & Southern Vietnam

- [Ho Chi Minh City Department of Tourism](https://vietnam.travel/) - Official municipal visitor guide for exploring District 1, the Saigon Opera House, and Ben Thanh Market.
- [Cu Chi Tunnels Historical Site Portal](https://vietnam.travel/) - Official visitor guide for the historic underground defense network located northwest of the city.

## Digital Nomad & Remote Work

Resources for long-term travelers, remote professionals, and digital nomads exploring Vietnam.

- [Vietnam Business and Remote Work Hubs (Da Nang & HCMC)](https://vietnam.travel/) - Official regional resources for coworking spaces and digital communities in major tech hubs.
- [Enouvo Space (Da Nang)](https://enouvospace.com/) - Popular community workspace and coliving hub catering to remote professionals and entrepreneurs.

## Safety & Emergency Services

Practical advice on emergency contact numbers, tourist assistance, and local safety protocols.

- [General Emergency Hotline (Police 113 / Fire 114 / Ambulance 115)](https://vietnam.travel/) - Direct national emergency dispatch numbers operational across all provinces.
